The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Pack my box with five dozen
liquor jugs. How vexingly quick daft zebras jump! Sphinx of black quartz,
judge my vow. The five boxing wizards jump quickly. Bright vixens jump; dozy
fowl quack. Jackdaws love my big sphinx of quartz. We promptly judged antique
ivory buckles for the next prize. A very bad quack might jinx zippy fowls.
Few quips galvanized the mock jury box. Heavy boxes perform waltzes; jinxed
sphinxes drum and prance. The jay, pig, fox, zebra and my wolves quack.
